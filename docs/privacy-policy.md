# Privacy Policy — Mi5G Prayer (مواقيتي)

_Last updated: 16 October 2026 · App id: com.midroid.prayertimes_

**Short version: the app collects nothing and sends nothing.**

- **No internet.** The app does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that collect data.**
- **Location (optional).** If you tap "Use my approximate location", Android gives the app an approximate
  (coarse) position. It is used **on your device only** to choose the nearest city and calculate prayer times and the
  Qibla direction. The coordinates are stored only in the app's private settings on your device. You can deny the
  permission and pick a city manually.
- **Notifications and alarms** are scheduled locally on your device.
- **Your own sound file.** If you choose a custom sound, the app gets read access to that one file through the
  Android file picker; it is played on your device and never copied or uploaded.
- **Compass sensors** are read only while the Qibla screen is open; nothing is stored.
- **Data deletion.** Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all settings.
- **Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone.
